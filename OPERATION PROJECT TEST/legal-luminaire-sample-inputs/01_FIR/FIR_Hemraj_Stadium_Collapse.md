### Brief Facts
On 17 March 2025, during a local district-level athletics event, a portion of the North Gallery seating area (approximately 12 metres length of masonry parapet and supporting columns) collapsed at about 18:55 hrs. Approximately 18–22 spectators and officials were present in that section. Consequence:

- Two persons sustained fatal injuries and were declared dead at SMS Hospital.
- Seven persons sustained grievous injuries (fractures, head injuries).
- Several others received simple injuries.

Preliminary inspection indicated that the collapsed portion was constructed/repaired in late 2022 – early 2023 using masonry mortar. The quality of the mortar appeared unusually weak and powdery. Samples of the collapsed mortar and adjoining intact masonry were collected, sealed, and dispatched to the State Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL), Jaipur.

The construction/repair work was allegedly executed by **M/s Hemraj Construction & Engineering Works** (Proprietor: Shri Hemraj Singh, C-45, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur) under a work order of the Stadium Management Committee.

### Action Taken
FIR registered. Investigation entrusted to SI (Investigation) Circle-II. Spot panchnama completed. FSL examination requested. Site sealed.
