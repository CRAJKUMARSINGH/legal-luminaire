SHOW CAUSE NOTICE
(Issued under the authority of Stadium Management Committee)

To,
Shri Hemraj Singh
Proprietor
M/s Hemraj Construction & Engineering Works
C-45, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur – 302017

Date: 22 March 2025
Reference: Work Order No. SMC/NG/2022/47 dated 15.11.2022 for repair of North Gallery parapet, Hemraj Memorial Stadium

Subject: Show Cause Notice regarding collapse of North Gallery parapet on 17.03.2025 and use of sub-standard materials

Sir,

It has come to the notice of the undersigned that a portion of the North Gallery parapet constructed/repaired by you under the above-mentioned work order collapsed on 17 March 2025 during an athletics event, resulting in two fatalities and several injuries.

Preliminary inspection and subsequent FSL examination indicate that the masonry mortar used was of deficient quality and did not conform to the applicable Indian Standards.

You are hereby called upon to show cause within seven (7) days of receipt of this notice as to why:
1. The security deposit and pending bills should not be forfeited;
2. Your firm should not be blacklisted from future works of the Stadium Management Committee and allied departments;
3. Appropriate legal action, including recovery of damages and criminal proceedings, should not be initiated against you.

Your written explanation, supported by documents (mix design, material test certificates, if any), must reach this office by 29 March 2025. Failure to reply will be treated as admission and further action will be taken without any additional opportunity.

This notice is issued without prejudice to any other rights and remedies available under law.

Sd/-
Secretary
Stadium Management Committee
Jaipur
Copy to: District Collector, SP (City), Executive Engineer (PWD)
