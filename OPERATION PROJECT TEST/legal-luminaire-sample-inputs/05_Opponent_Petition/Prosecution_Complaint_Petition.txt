COMPLAINT / POLICE REPORT UNDER SECTION 193 BNSS

Most respectfully showeth:

1. That the complainant is the State of Rajasthan represented through the Investigating Officer of PS Civil Lines, Jaipur.

2. That on 17.03.2025 a portion of the North Gallery of Hemraj Memorial Stadium collapsed, resulting in the death of two persons and grievous injuries to seven others.

3. That investigation has revealed that the accused, being the contractor who had undertaken the repair work of the said gallery in 2022-23, used sub-standard masonry mortar which did not conform to the prescribed Indian Standards (as confirmed by FSL Report No. FSL/JP/CIVIL/2025/0876 dated 12.05.2025).

4. That the accused thereby committed offences punishable under Section 105 BNSS (causing death by negligence) and Section 125 BNSS (endangering life or personal safety of others).

5. That the accused was arrested on 05.06.2025 and is presently on bail.

6. That the charge sheet along with all relevant documents and list of witnesses has been filed.

PRAYER
It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon’ble Court may be pleased to:
a) Take cognizance of the offences;
b) Summon the accused;
c) Try the accused according to law and award appropriate punishment;
d) Pass any other order(s) as this Hon’ble Court may deem fit and proper.
