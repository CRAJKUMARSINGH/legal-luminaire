Brief Facts of the Case (as per investigation):
Investigation has established that the accused, as the contractor who executed the repair/reconstruction of the North Gallery parapet of Hemraj Memorial Stadium in the year 2022-23, used sub-standard masonry mortar. The FSL report dated 12.05.2025 (FSL/JP/CIVIL/2025/0876) confirms that the mortar did not meet the prescribed standards and was a probable cause of the collapse on 17.03.2025 resulting in two deaths and multiple injuries.

Statements of the complainant, injured witnesses, Stadium Engineer, and PWD officials have been recorded. Site plan, panchnama, medical reports of deceased and injured, and FSL report form part of the charge sheet.

List of Witnesses: 14 (including complainant, doctors, FSL expert, Stadium officials).
List of Documents: FIR, Panchnama, FSL Report, Medical Post-mortem reports, Work Order, Measurement Book extracts, etc.
