Brief Facts (as narrated by complainant):

On 17 March 2025, during a local district-level athletics event, a portion of the North Gallery seating area (approximately 12 metres length of masonry parapet and supporting columns) collapsed at about 18:55 hrs. Approximately 18–22 spectators and officials were present in that section. As a result:

- Two persons (identity under verification) sustained fatal injuries and were declared dead at SMS Hospital.
- Seven persons sustained grievous injuries (fractures, head injuries).
- Several others received simple injuries.

Preliminary inspection by the Stadium Engineer and local PWD official present at the site indicated that the collapsed portion was constructed/ repaired in late 2022 – early 2023 using masonry mortar. The complainant states that the quality of the mortar appeared unusually weak and powdery. Samples of the collapsed mortar and adjoining intact masonry were collected by the local police and sealed in the presence of witnesses for onward transmission to the State Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL), Jaipur.

The construction / repair work was allegedly executed by M/s Hemraj Construction & Engineering Works (proprietor: Shri Hemraj Singh, resident of C-45, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur) under a work order issued by the Stadium Management Committee.

No immediate arrest. Investigation continues. Site has been sealed. FSL samples dispatched under proper seal on 18 March 2025.

Action Taken: FIR registered. Investigation entrusted to SI (Investigation) Circle-II. Spot panchnama, statements of injured and witnesses being recorded. Request for FSL examination of mortar samples already moved.
