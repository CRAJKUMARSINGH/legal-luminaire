Subject: Examination report of masonry mortar samples collected from the site of gallery collapse at Hemraj Memorial Stadium, Jaipur.

1. DESCRIPTION OF SAMPLES
   - Sample A: Collapsed masonry mortar from North Gallery parapet (sealed packet marked “A”)
   - Sample B: Intact adjoining masonry mortar (sealed packet marked “B”)
   - Sample C: Control sample of fresh mortar mix claimed to be used (if any) – not received

2. TESTS CONDUCTED
   The samples were examined for compressive strength, consistency, and composition in accordance with the relevant Indian Standards for concrete and mortar.

3. OBSERVATIONS & RESULTS
   - Visual: Sample A showed high porosity, poor bonding, and powdery residue on light abrasion.
   - Compressive strength (28-day equivalent estimated): Sample A – 1.8 N/mm² (very low); Sample B – 4.2 N/mm².
   - The mortar in Sample A does not meet the minimum requirements prescribed under **IS 1199:2018 (Methods of sampling and analysis of concrete)** for structural applications.
   - Cement:sand ratio appeared leaner than 1:6.
   - No evidence of deliberate adulteration with non-cementitious material was conclusively established, but workmanship and mix proportion appear deficient.

4. OPINION
   The masonry mortar used in the collapsed portion (Sample A) is of sub-standard quality and does not conform to the specifications under IS 1199:2018. This deficiency is a probable contributing factor to the structural failure of the gallery parapet under normal load conditions present during the event.

5. This report is issued for the purpose of investigation only. The samples remain in the custody of FSL until further orders.
