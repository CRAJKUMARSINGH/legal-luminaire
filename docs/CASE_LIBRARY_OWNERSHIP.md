## Ownership Responsibilities

### Primary Duties
1. **Quality Assurance**: Ensure all cases in vertical meet Case Pack Standard
2. **Integration**: Manage demo browser integration for vertical cases
3. **Duplication Resolution**: Identify and resolve overlaps with other verticals
4. **Documentation**: Maintain vertical-specific guidelines and precedents
5. **Review Process**: Review and approve new case additions within vertical

### Review Process
1. New case packs must be submitted to vertical owner for review
2. Owner checks against Case Pack Standard and quality gates
3. Owner identifies duplication risks with other verticals
4. Owner approves/rejects with specific feedback
5. Approved cases are integrated by owner into main system

### Escalation Path
- **Vertical-level issues**: Resolve within vertical
- **Cross-vertical duplication**: Escalate to Recovery Lead
- **Standard violations**: Escalate to Recovery Lead
- **Technical integration issues**: Coordinate with technical team
