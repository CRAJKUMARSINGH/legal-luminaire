## Marketing Scenarios

### 1. Real Estate Law Firm Demo
**Target Audience:** Real estate law firms, property developers
**Primary Cases:** TC-74 (Specific Performance), TC-75 (Injunction), TC-62 (Title + Mutation)
**Demo Flow:**
1. Property dispute intake
2. Title verification and analysis
3. Agreement drafting and review
4. Injunction application for urgent relief
5. Specific performance suit preparation
6. Bilingual document generation

### 2. Banking Sector Solution Demo
**Target Audience:** Banks, financial institutions, NBFCs
**Primary Cases:** TC-32 (Bank Fraud), TC-42 (Cheque Dishonour), TC-35 (Money Laundering)
**Demo Flow:**
1. Fraud case intake and classification
2. Evidence analysis and Fact-Fit Gate
3. Legal notice generation
4. Complaint drafting and filing
5. Regulatory compliance checking
6. Status tracking and reporting

### 3. Criminal Defense Practice Demo
**Target Audience:** Criminal law firms, defense attorneys
**Primary Cases:** TC-27 (Murder), TC-30 (POCSO), TC-52 (Cheating 420)
**Demo Flow:**
1. Criminal case intake
2. Charge sheet analysis
3. Evidence matrix preparation
4. Defense strategy planning
5. Bail application drafting
6. Trial preparation and arguments

### 4. Corporate Legal Department Demo
**Target Audience:** Corporate legal teams, compliance officers
**Primary Cases:** TC-37 (ACB Trap), TC-77 (Partnership Dispute), TC-84 (Consumer)
**Demo Flow:**
1. Corporate dispute intake
2. Risk assessment and compliance check
3. Internal investigation support
4. Legal documentation generation
5. Regulatory reporting
6. Case management and tracking

### 5. Government Sector Demo
**Target Audience:** Government legal departments, public prosecutors
**Primary Cases:** TC-23 (Road Arbitration), TC-39 (Disproportionate Assets), TC-65 (Revenue Records)
**Demo Flow:**
1. Government project dispute handling
2. Public servant case management
3. Revenue record integration
4. Compliance documentation
5. Reporting and analytics
6. Inter-department coordination
