## 🚀 Specialized Criminal Law Features

### Feature 1: "The Dying Declaration Analyzer"
**Value**: Dying declarations are critical in murder cases. Our AI checks:
- Was the declaration recorded by a magistrate?
- Was the declarant fit to make the statement?
- Was it recorded in the declarant's language?
- Were there corroborating circumstances?

### Feature 2: "The Last-Seen Theory Evaluator"
**Value**: Last-seen theory is the foundation of many murder convictions. Our AI evaluates:
- Time gap between last seen and discovery of body
- Route and distance analysis
- Possibility of alternative explanations
- Consistency with other evidence

### Feature 3: "The POCSO Age Determination Tool"
**Value**: Age determination is critical in POCSO cases. Our AI analyzes:
- School certificates vs ossification test
- Expert opinion reliability
- Presumption under POCSO Act
- Benefit of doubt considerations

### Feature 4: "The Conspiracy Chain Analyzer"
**Value**: Conspiracy (Section 120B) requires common intention. Our AI checks:
- Common intention vs same intention
- Overt acts attributed to each accused
- Meeting of minds evidence
- Separate roles vs joint participation

## 🔍 Comparative Analysis: Criminal vs Civil Compendium

### Criminal Law Specific Challenges:
1. **Higher Stakes**: Liberty at risk, not just property
2. **Forensic Evidence**: Technical FSL reports require specialized knowledge
3. **Strict Deadlines**: Remand limits, charge-sheet filing — no extensions
4. **Procedural Complexity**: BNSS procedures are complex and strictly enforced
5. **Public Interest**: Prosecution has state resources, defense needs systematic approach

### Civil Law Specific Challenges:
1. **Document Volume**: More documents, longer proceedings
2. **Valuation Issues**: Court fee calculation, claim valuation
3. **Multiple Forums**: Civil court, consumer forum, RERA — overlapping jurisdiction
4. **Limitation Issues**: Longer limitation periods, more complex calculation
5. **Easement/Property Rights**: Complex property law concepts

### Legal Luminaire's Approach:
- **Criminal**: FSL audit + discharge grounds + bail arguments + deadline tracking
- **Civil**: Template library + evidence matrix + draft retrieval + court fee calculator
- **Both**: Fact-Fit Gate + verification + bilingual output + zero hallucinations
