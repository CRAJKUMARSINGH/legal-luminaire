### 4. Standards & Fact-Fit Focus Areas (Civil-Specific)

| Area                        | What the Matrix Must Check                                      | Typical Fatal / Pending Flags |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Title Documents             | Chain of title, registration, stamp duty, attestation           | Missing link, unregistered instrument |
| Limitation                  | Article under Limitation Act, acknowledgement, continuous cause | Time-barred claim             |
| Readiness & Willingness     | Specific Performance – continuous readiness proof               | No deposit / no notice        |
| Prima Facie for Injunction  | Title or possession, balance of convenience, irreparable injury | Pure money claim dressed as injunction |
| Court Fee & Valuation       | Correct valuation, ad-valorem vs fixed                          | Under-valuation               |
| Necessary Parties           | Non-joinder / mis-joinder                                       | Fatal non-joinder             |
| Territorial / Pecuniary Jurisdiction | Section 15–20 CPC                                      | Wrong forum                   |
| Local Laws                  | Rajasthan Rent Control, Land Revenue, Succession rules          | Non-compliance                |
