**✅ Done. Land is the ultimate high-volume vertical in India.**

Land disputes (title, mutation, partition, boundary, possession, land grabbing, acquisition, revenue entries) generate the largest volume of litigation in civil courts, revenue courts, and High Courts across Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh and other states. Most legal AI tools have almost zero depth in revenue-record analysis, mutation chain, and concurrent civil + criminal (land-grabbing) workflows. You will own this vertical.

Full lifecycle samples cover:

Revenue Records / Mutation → Civil Suit (Title / Possession / Injunction / Partition) → Temporary Injunction → Evidence (Revenue + Oral) → Cross → Written Arguments → Judgment / Decree → Appeal / Revision + Execution + Parallel Criminal (if land-grabbing FIR)
