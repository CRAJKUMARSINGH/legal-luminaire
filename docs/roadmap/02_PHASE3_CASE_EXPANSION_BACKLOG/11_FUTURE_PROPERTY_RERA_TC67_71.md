**✅ Done. Property is the twin of land — high-value civil + hybrid vertical.**

Property disputes (specific performance of sale agreements, permanent injunction, declaration of title to built-up property, landlord-tenant, mortgage, easement, builder-buyer, and related criminal overlays such as criminal breach of trust / cheating on property) form a massive docket in civil courts and consumer forums. Combined with land packs, you cover the entire “immovable property” universe for Indian practitioners.

Full lifecycle:

Agreement to Sell / Sale Deed → Suit for Specific Performance / Injunction / Declaration → Temporary Injunction / Status Quo → Evidence (documents + oral) → Cross → Arguments → Judgment / Decree → Appeal + Execution + Parallel Consumer / Criminal if needed.
