### Citation Safety & Verification
- Fact-Fit Gate for precedent scoring
- Multi-tier citation verification (Court-Safe, Verified, Secondary, Pending, Fatal Error)
- Hard citation block for unsafe references
- Five verification tiers before drafting

### AI-Powered Tools
- Grounded Legal Copilot with source-linked answers
- AI Legal Research through structured workflows
- Citation research with factual context preservation

### Drafting & Case Management
- Structured legal draft generation
- Bilingual drafting (Hindi & English)
- Chronology Studio for timeline building
- Deadline Engine for limitation tracking
- Pre-filing checklist for human review

### Knowledge & Standards
- Standards Explorer for Indian engineering and legal standards
- Accuracy Academy for AI trade-off education
- 26 synthetic demo cases across multiple practice areas
