# Pre-Filing Checklist – Specific Performance Suit

- [ ] Original Agreement to Sell + all annexures reviewed
- [ ] Proof of payment of earnest / part consideration (cheque clearance)
- [ ] Continuous readiness evidence collected (DD, bank letter, presence proof)
- [ ] Legal notices issued and service proof available
- [ ] Title chain of seller examined – gaps flagged in Evidence Matrix
- [ ] Subsequent purchaser details obtained (if any)
- [ ] Limitation calculation under Article 54 verified
- [ ] Court fee calculated on consideration / market value
- [ ] Necessary parties joined (seller + subsequent purchaser)
- [ ] Schedule of property accurate
- [ ] Fact-Fit Gate completed – PENDING / FATAL items blocked or qualified
- [ ] Order 39 application prepared with supporting affidavit
- [ ] Bilingual versions (English + Hindi) ready
- [ ] Client verification & final approval taken
