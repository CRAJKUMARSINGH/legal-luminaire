**✅ TC-75 Full Set – Permanent + Temporary Injunction**

**Folder**: `CIVIL_04_INJUNCTION_2026`  
**Title**: Suit for Permanent Injunction + Application under Order 39 Rules 1 & 2  
**Court**: Civil Judge / Additional District Judge, Udaipur (Rajasthan)  
**Synthetic Case**: Threatened construction / interference with easement and peaceful possession of residential property.

**Parties**  
- Plaintiff: Smt. Kamla Devi (owner in possession)  
- Defendant: Shri Vikram Singh (neighbour / adjoining plot owner)

**Core Dispute**  
Plaintiff is absolute owner and in peaceful possession of a residential house with a 10-ft wide side passage used as easement of necessity / right of way for over 20 years. Defendant has started digging foundation and stacking construction material with the clear intention of blocking the passage and raising a wall that will obstruct light, air and access. Plaintiff seeks permanent injunction restraining the Defendant from interfering with possession and easement, and temporary injunction under Order 39 to maintain status quo during the pendency of the suit.

**Key Legal Issues**  
- Prima facie title / possession  
- Balance of convenience  
- Irreparable injury  
- Easement by prescription / necessity  
- Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 CPC principles  
- Local inspection / commissioner report  

**Defence Theme**  
Defendant claims the passage is not an easement, that Plaintiff has no title to the strip, and that construction is within his own boundary. He also raises delay and clean-hands arguments.
