# Standards Matrix – Temporary & Permanent Injunction

| Area                        | Applicable Standard                                      | Status / Notes |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|----------------|
| Prima facie case            | Order 39 R.1 & 2 + settled SC principles                 | Strong – title + possession + interference proved |
| Balance of convenience      | Comparative mischief test                                | Favours Plaintiff – loss of access vs temporary halt of construction |
| Irreparable injury          | Injury not adequately compensable in money               | Access, light, air – classic irreparable |
| Clean hands / delay         | Equitable principles                                     | No delay; notice issued promptly |
| Easement                    | Indian Easements Act – prescription / necessity          | User > 20 years pleaded |
| Local inspection            | Order 39 R.7 / Commissioner                              | Recommended for demo |
| Permanent injunction        | Specific Relief Act ss. 38–41                            | Available once title/possession established |
