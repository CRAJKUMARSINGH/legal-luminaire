## Key Legal Standards

### Section 307 BNS (Attempt to Murder)
- Requires intention to kill or knowledge that act is likely to cause death
- Distinction from hurt: nature of injury, weapon used, part of body injured
- Supreme Court: State vs. Prakash (2021) - factors to determine intent

### Medical Evidence Standards
- Injury certificate must be from registered medical practitioner
- Certificate should specify: nature of injury, weapon likely used, danger to life
- Classification of injuries: simple, grievous, dangerous

### Self-Defence (Section 96-106 IPC / BNS)
- Right of private defence available when there is reasonable apprehension
- Force used must be proportionate to the threat
- Burden of proof on accused to establish self-defence
