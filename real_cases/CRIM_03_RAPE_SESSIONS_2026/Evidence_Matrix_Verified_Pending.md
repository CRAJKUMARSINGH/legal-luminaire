## Verification Tier Definitions

- **VERIFIED**: Documentally proven with high reliability
- **COURT_SAFE**: Generally accepted by courts unless challenged
- **SECONDARY**: Requires corroboration, weak standing alone
- **PENDING**: Insufficient information to verify
- **FATAL_ERROR**: Critical procedural defect that may invalidate evidence
