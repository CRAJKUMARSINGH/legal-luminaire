## Key Legal Standards

### Section 376 BNS (Rape)
- Definition of rape under new criminal law
- Consent: affirmative, voluntary, conscious agreement
- Supreme Court: State vs. Pramod (2022) - consent must be informed and voluntary
- Marriage promise cases: distinction between false promise and breach of promise

### Medical Evidence in Rape Cases
- Medical examination must be conducted within prescribed timeline
- Examination should document: injuries, DNA samples, clothing
- Absence of injuries does not disprove rape
- Medical Board guidelines for collection of evidence

### 164 BNSS Statement
- Must be recorded by Magistrate
- Statement should be recorded in camera
- Victim's comfort and dignity must be maintained
- Statement can be used as evidence if victim turns hostile

### Delay in FIR
- Supreme Court: Delay in filing FIR in rape cases is not fatal
- Court must examine reason for delay
- Explanation must be satisfactory
- Delay cannot be sole ground for acquittal
