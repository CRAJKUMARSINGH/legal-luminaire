## Key Legal Standards

### POCSO Act Provisions

**Section 4 (Penetrative Sexual Assault)**
- Minimum punishment: 10 years imprisonment
- Can extend to life imprisonment
- No provision for lesser punishment

**Section 5 (Aggravated Penetrative Sexual Assault)**
- When accused is in position of trust (teacher, guardian)
- Minimum punishment: 20 years imprisonment
- Can extend to death penalty

**Section 6 (Punishment for Aggravated Penetrative Sexual Assault)**
- Stricter punishment for aggravated cases
- No provision for bail under certain circumstances

**Section 19 (Mandatory Reporting)**
- Every person who has knowledge of offence must report
- Failure to report is punishable
- Protects child from further abuse

**Section 21 (Procedure for Reporting)**
- Report to be made within 24 hours
- Specific format for reporting
- Protection of victim's identity

### Age Determination Standards

**Supreme Court Guidelines**
- School certificate is primary evidence of age
- Ossification test is secondary and can only indicate age range
- Birth certificate is most reliable but not always available
- If documents conflict, court must determine most reliable

**POCSO Act Specifics**
- Presumption of minority if victim appears to be child
- Burden of proof on accused to prove victim is major
- Court can order age determination from medical board

### Child-Friendly Procedures

**In-Camera Trial**
- Trial conducted in private
- No public allowed
- Only concerned persons present
- Protects child from trauma

**Support Person**
- Child must have support person during testimony
- Can be parent, guardian, or social worker
- Helps child feel comfortable

**No Repeated Testimony**
- Child's statement recorded once
- Cross-examination conducted carefully
- No aggressive questioning

### Compensation under POCSO

**Section 33(8)**
- Special Court can order compensation
- Based on Victim Compensation Scheme
- Interim relief possible
- Rehabilitation and support services
