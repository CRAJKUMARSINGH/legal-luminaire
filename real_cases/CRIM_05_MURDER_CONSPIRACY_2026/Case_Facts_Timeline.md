## Case Overview

**Case Title**: State vs. Vijay Singh & Others  
**Court**: Rajasthan High Court, Jaipur  
**FIR No.:** 567/2025, P.S. Jaipur  
**Sections**: 302, 120B, 201 BNS (Murder, Conspiracy, Destruction of Evidence)  
**Date of Incident**: 15 July 2025  

**Accused**:  
- A1 – Vijay Singh (main accused, business rival)  
- A2 – Rahul Sharma (alleged conspirator)  
- A3 – Suresh Kumar (alleged conspirator)  

**Deceased**: Mahesh Kumar, aged 45, businessman

**Core Allegation**: The accused conspired to murder the deceased due to business rivalry. They allegedly met, planned the murder, executed it, and then destroyed evidence by dumping the body in a canal.

**Defence Theme**: No direct evidence of conspiracy. Circumstantial evidence weak. No witness to the conspiracy meeting. Call records don't prove conspiracy. False implication due to business dispute.
