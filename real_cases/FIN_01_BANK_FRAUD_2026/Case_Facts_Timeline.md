## Case Overview

**Case Title**: State vs. Vijay Kumar Sharma  
**Court**: Sessions Court, Jaipur (Rajasthan)  
**FIR No.:** 789/2025, P.S. Economic Offences Wing, Jaipur  
**Sections**: 420, 406, 409, 120B BNS  
**Date of Incident**: 10 January 2025 onwards  

**Accused**: Vijay Kumar Sharma (Director of M/s. Sharma Enterprises)

**Complainant**: Branch Manager, State Bank of India, Jaipur

**Core Allegation**: The accused obtained a loan of ₹5 crores from the bank using forged documents, false financial statements, and fake collateral. Funds were diverted to personal accounts and shell companies instead of the stated business purpose.

**Defence Theme**: Civil dispute dressed as criminal. No dishonest intention at inception. Genuine business setbacks. Repayment efforts made. Documents were genuine but bank misinterpreted them.
