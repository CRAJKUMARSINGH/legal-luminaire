## Key Legal Standards

### Section 420 BNS (Cheating)
- Ingredients: deception, dishonest intention, delivery of property
- Supreme Court: Mens rea at inception is critical
- Distinction between civil breach and criminal cheating
- Burden of proof on prosecution to establish dishonest intention

### Section 406 BNS (Criminal Breach of Trust)
- Ingredients: entrustment, dishonest misappropriation
- Distinction from civil breach of contract
- Supreme Court: Must be established that property was entrusted
- Dominant intention test for classification

### Section 409 BNS (Criminal Breach of Trust by Public Servant)
- Not applicable here (accused is private person)
- Included for reference in related cases
