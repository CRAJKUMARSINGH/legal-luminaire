## Case Overview

**Case Title**: State vs. Rajesh Kumar Gupta  
**Court**: Sessions Court, Udaipur (Rajasthan)  
**FIR No.:** 456/2025, P.S. Economic Offences Wing, Udaipur  
**Sections**: 420, 406 BNS  
**Date of Incident**: 1 February 2025 onwards  

**Accused**: Rajesh Kumar Gupta (Director of M/s. Golden Returns Investment Ltd.)

**Complainant**: Group of 50 investors

**Core Allegation**: The accused launched a collective investment scheme promising 25% monthly returns, collected ₹2 crores from 50 investors, and failed to pay returns or return principal. The scheme was not registered with SEBI.

**Defence Theme**: Genuine business losses. No dishonest intention. Returns delayed due to market conditions. Some investors already repaid. Civil matter, not criminal.
