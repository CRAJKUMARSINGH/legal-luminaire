## Key Legal Precedents & Standards

### 1. SEBI Collective Investment Schemes (CIS)
- **Regulation 3(4) Exclusion**: Schemes in nature of partnership or bona fide commercial contracts where investors retain direct operational rights do not constitute CIS.
- **SEBI v. Gaurav Varshney (2016)**: Regulatory violation under SEBI Act does not automatically constitute Section 420 criminal cheating without independent proof of deceit at inception.

### 2. Cheating vs Commercial Breach
- **Hridaya Ranjan Pd. Verma v. State of Bihar (2000) 4 SCC 168**: Merely failing to keep a promise or pay anticipated profits is not cheating unless dishonest intention existed at the time of making the promise.
- **Vesa Holdings (P) Ltd. v. State of Kerala (2015) 8 SCC 293**: Every breach of contract does not give rise to criminal prosecution for cheating.

### 3. Bail Considerations in Economic Offences
- **P. Chidambaram v. Directorate of Enforcement (2020) 13 SCC 791**: Economic offences are serious, but bail is the rule; flight risk, tampering, and witness intimidation are the governing criteria.
- **Sanjay Chandra v. CBI (2012) 1 SCC 40**: Length of pre-trial detention cannot be punitive when trial is likely to take prolonged duration.
