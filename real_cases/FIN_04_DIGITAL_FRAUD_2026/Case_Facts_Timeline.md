## Case Overview
- **Case Title**: State of Rajasthan vs. Amit Verma
- **Court**: Court of Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate (Cyber Crimes), Jaipur
- **FIR No.**: 48/2025, P.S. Cyber Crime Police Station, Jaipur
- **Sections**: Sections 66C, 66D Information Technology Act, 2000 + Section 420, 468 IPC (Sections 318, 336 BNS)
- **Accused**: Amit Verma (Software Support Executive)
- **Complainant**: Senior citizen victim of unauthorized banking session hijacking
- **Core Allegation**: Accused allegedly operated phishing gateway and received compromised banking OTP credentials to divert funds.
- **Defence Theme**: Total electronic evidence failure. CFSL forensic analysis found zero phishing APK remnants or compromised bank credentials on accused device; dynamic IP address was shared via carrier-grade NAT (CGNAT) without port-forwarding verification; mandatory Section 65B Certificate absent.
