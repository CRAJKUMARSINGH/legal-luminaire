## Case Overview
- **Case Title**: State of Rajasthan vs. Dr. S. N. Meena & Ramesh Chandra
- **Court**: Sessions Court, Kota (Rajasthan)
- **FIR No.**: 89/2025, P.S. Nayapura, Kota
- **Statutory Sections**: Sections 420, 468, 471, 120B IPC / Sections 318, 336, 340 BNS
- **Accused**: Ramesh Chandra (Insured Patient) & Dr. S. N. Meena (Surgeon, Lifeline Hospital)
- **Complainant**: Star Health / ICICI Lombard General Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Core Allegation**: Allegation of fraudulent staged hospitalization and bogus billing to defraud insurance company.
- **Defence Theme**: Genuine hospitalization attested by treating surgeon, OT nursing staff, biometric hospital attendance logs, pathology histology specimens, and post-operative ultrasound scans. The insurance investigator's ex-parte hearsay opinion cannot override contemporaneous medical records.
