## Key Governing Standards & Judgments
1. **Jacob Mathew v. State of Punjab (2005) 6 SCC 1**: Criminal prosecution against medical practitioners requires gross negligence or clear dishonest intention; clinical records carry presumption of regular performance of medical duties.
2. **National Insurance Co. Ltd. v. Nitin Khandelwal (2008) 11 SCC 259**: Genuine hospitalization supported by treating medical professionals cannot be categorized as criminal fraud based solely on surveyor skepticism.
