# TC-74: Specific Performance - Civil Court (Readiness & Willingness)

**Case ID**: TC-74  
**Folder**: `CIVIL_03_SPECIFIC_PERFORMANCE_2026`  
**Court**: Civil Court, Kota (Rajasthan)  
**Relief**: Specific performance of agreement to sell  
**Key Legal Issues**: Section 16 SRA, time essence, readiness & willingness, subsequent purchaser

## Case Overview

Agreement to sell residential plot for ₹85,00,000. Plaintiff paid advance, remained ready to pay balance. Defendant failed to execute sale deed and instead sold to third party. Suit for specific performance with alternative damages claim.
