## Case Overview

Suit for permanent injunction to restrain defendant from constructing on plaintiff's easement right. Application for temporary injunction under Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 to maintain status quo during litigation. Threat of irreparable injury to plaintiff's light and air rights.
