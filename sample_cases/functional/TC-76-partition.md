# TC-76: Partition (Preliminary + Final) - Civil Court (Metes & Bounds)

**Case ID**: TC-76  
**Folder**: `CIVIL_05_PARTITION_2026`  
**Court**: Civil Court, Jaipur (Rajasthan)  
**Relief**: Partition of ancestral property + accounts  
**Key Legal Issues**: Metes & bounds, preliminary decree, final decree, accounts

## Case Overview

Suit for partition of ancestral agricultural land among 4 co-parceners. Property description requires metes and bounds. Prayer for preliminary decree declaring shares, followed by final decree with actual partition by commissioner. Accounts of income and expenses sought.
