# TC-82: Succession/Probate/Letters of Administration - Civil Court (Indian Succession Act)

**Case ID**: TC-82  
**Folder**: `CIVIL_11_SUCCESSION_2026`  
**Court**: Civil Court, Delhi  
**Relief**: Probate / Letters of Administration  
**Key Legal Issues**: Indian Succession Act, will challenge, caveatable interest

## Case Overview

Petition for grant of probate of will. Will dated 2018 bequeaths property to plaintiff. Legal heirs challenge will on grounds of fraud, undue influence, and lack of testamentary capacity. Plaintiff seeks probate with powers of administration. Caveat filed by objectors.
