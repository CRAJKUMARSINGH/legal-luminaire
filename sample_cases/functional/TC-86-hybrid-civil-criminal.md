# TC-86: Hybrid Civil + Criminal Overlay - Civil Court + Sessions Court

**Case ID**: TC-86  
**Folder**: `CIVIL_15_HYBRID_CIVIL_CRIMINAL_2026`  
**Court**: Civil Court + Sessions Court (Concurrent)  
**Relief**: Civil possession + criminal prosecution  
**Key Legal Issues**: Stay of civil/criminal, concurrent findings, CBT FIR

## Case Overview

Hybrid case with civil suit for possession and parallel criminal FIR for criminal breach of trust and cheating over land grabbing. Plaintiff seeks stay of criminal proceedings pending civil suit, or vice versa. Demonstration of concurrent civil-criminal proceedings and principles for stay.
