# 02 — Facilitator Protocol

## Setup

One cohort, three to six people, mixed levels but grouped by track. One
facilitator who has run contested Rajasthan matters. One rule: no one sees an
`expected` block in the room.

## Two annexes you run from

`annex/D` is the one-line answer key. `annex/F` is the trap index, ordered by
week. Read the week's F block before the week and keep it open during marking.
Neither annex goes to the trainee.

## Before the cohort

1. Read all twenty-four pairs, `annex/D-facilitator-answer-key.md` and
   `annex/F-trap-index.md`.
2. Print or copy the `input` block of each week's pair into a separate sheet.
   Strip the `expected` block entirely.
3. Confirm the app runs and the pairs are on the branch.

## Running a session

- **Do not teach first.** Session 1 is silent writing, 25 minutes, no questions
  answered. Answer only "what does this word mean".
- **Collect before discussing.** Take the sheets in. Do not let anyone read
  another's answer until marking.
- **Session 2 is prediction only.** Trainees write their predicted gates on the
  same sheet. Still no expected block.
- **Session 3 is the reveal.** Hand out the `expected` block. Mark against the
  Rubric in pairs, not alone.
- **Session 4 is a rewrite.** Everyone rewrites to the next band up. The
  rewrite is the deliverable, not the cold attempt.

## Marking

Mark the cold attempt and the rewrite separately. The cold attempt is diagnostic
— a low band there is information, not failure. The rewrite is what counts.

A trainee who scores band 2 cold and band 4 on rewrite has learned the week. A
trainee who scores band 4 cold and band 4 on rewrite has coasted.

## Difficult cases

**The confident wrong answer.** Some trainees will produce a fluent, wrong
document. Do not correct it verbally. Point at the gate they missed and let the
marking do the work. Fluency is the hardest thing to unlearn.

**The paralysed trainee.** Writes nothing, fears the gate. Give a 15-minute
timer and permission to be wrong in writing. `PENDING` is a valid answer.

**The over-rider.** Writes a better answer than the engine. Good. Have them
argue it in session 4 and, if it holds, open a PR against `seniorVariants`.

## Facilitator errors to avoid

- Revealing the gate early "to save time". It always costs more time later.
- Marking for prose quality. Mark the gate, the clock and the terminal outcome.
- Skipping session 4 when the cohort is behind. Session 4 is where the learning
  consolidates; session 1 is where it is diagnosed.
- Letting a trainee write `filing-ready`. Nothing is filing-ready.

## Weekly debrief (10 minutes, facilitator only)

Record: cohort band distribution, the most common failure mode, and how many of
the week's indexed traps actually caught someone. If fewer than half caught
anyone, the cold prompt is too soft — note it and sharpen it for the next
cohort.
