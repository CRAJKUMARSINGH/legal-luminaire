# 03 — Rubric

Five bands. Mark each of the seven criteria. Average to a band. Half-bands allowed.

## Criteria

1. **Gate accuracy** — did you predict the citation and limitation gates, and the
   terminal outcome kind, correctly?
2. **Forum correctness** — is the forum right, and is the reason right?
3. **Clock handling** — is the operative date identified and the deadline worked
   from the correct anchor?
4. **Document discipline** — is what you requisitioned, or refused to draft,
   correct?
5. **Adverse-fact retention** — did you keep the fact that hurts the client?
6. **Language** — plain, accurate, no translated-Hindi, no borrowed flourish.
7. **Oral discipline** (weeks 10–11 only) — was the examination, the seating
   and the accommodation prepared for a child, not for a bench?

## Bands

**Band 1 — Wrong and fluent.**
Confident document, wrong forum or wrong clock, adverse fact dropped. Would have
been filed and returned, or filed and lost.

**Band 2 — Right instinct, wrong execution.**
Gates roughly right, one hard error (wrong anchor date, missing requisition,
adverse fact softened). Would survive a supervisor but cost a week.

**Band 3 — Competent.**
Correct forum, correct clock, gates broadly right, adverse fact kept but
underweighted. Filable after supervision.

**Band 4 — Strong.**
Everything correct, plus one thing the engine did not do: a second relief, a
pre-empted objection, a better requisition. Would go to a supervisor with
confidence.

**Band 5 — Supervisor standard.**
Correct, complete, adverse fact turned into a strength, and the strategic fork
named explicitly rather than resolved silently.

## Band anchors

- Band 1 cold is normal in weeks 1–3. Band 1 at week 8 is a fail.
- Band 3 is the pass mark for the course.
- Band 5 cold is rare and usually means the trainee has already read the pair.
  Ask.

## Weeks 10–11 adjustment

Drop criterion 3 (clock handling) to half weight. Add criterion 7 at full
weight. A POCSO file is not lost to a deadline; it is lost to a leading
question and to a screen nobody asked for.

## What never earns marks

- Length.
- Case law without a paragraph reference.
- Section numbers without a `PENDING` tag where you are unsure.
- The words "filing-ready", "watertight", "strong case" without a reason.
