# Annex A — Rajasthan Forum Map

Working map, not an authority. Verify every entry against the current
establishment list before filing. Marked where uncertain.

## 1. Civil courts

- Civil Judge (Junior Division) — pecuniary limit set by the Rajasthan Civil
  Courts Ordinance 1950; **limit unverified, confirm**.
- Civil Judge (Senior Division) — above that limit.
- District Judge — appeals from Civil Judge (JD), and original matters assigned.
- High Court — Rajasthan HC, Jodhpur (principal seat) and Jaipur (bench).

## 2. Criminal courts

- CJM / ACJM / JMFC — trial of summons and warrant cases.
- Chief Judicial Magistrate — remand, and certain special matters.
- Sessions Judge / Additional Sessions Judge — sessions triable offences,
  including 302, 307, 376, POCSO, SC/ST Act.
- Special Courts — POCSO, SC/ST Act, NDPS, PCA, MP/MLA.
- High Court — bail under s. 483 BNSS, appeals under s. 415, revision under
  s. 438, inherent power under s. 528.

## 3. Revenue courts

- Naib Tehsildar / Tehsildar — first instance in many tenancy matters.
- Sub-Divisional Officer — appellate authority in some classes.
- Collector — appellate and revisional.
- Divisional Commissioner.
- Rajasthan Revenue Board, Ajmer — highest revenue authority.
- **Bar of civil jurisdiction**: Rajasthan Tenancy Act 1955 s. 207, subject to
  the exceptions in the Act. Never plead the bar or its absence without reading
  s. 207 and the exception provisions together.

## 4. Specialised forums

- Family Court — matrimonial and maintenance matters; BNSS s. 144 applications.
- Commercial Court — specified commercial disputes above the threshold.
- MACT — motor accident claims.
- Rent Tribunal / Appellate Rent Tribunal — Rajasthan Rent Control Act 2001.
- Rajasthan RERA Authority, Jaipur — promoter-allottee disputes.
- Consumer Commission (District / State / National).
- NGT (Pune bench for Rajasthan) — environmental.
- Debt Recovery Tribunal, Jaipur.

## 5. High Court bench allocation

Jaipur bench serves the Jaipur division districts; Jodhpur the rest. A Jaipur
division appeal goes to the Jaipur bench, not the principal seat. **Confirm the
current district allocation from the HC's own cause-list circular.**

## 6. Legal aid and support

- Rajasthan State Legal Services Authority (RALSA).
- District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) in every district.
- Jail legal aid clinics — the route for a custodial client's vakalatnama.
- Rajasthan State Bar Council for a fee or misconduct reference.

## 7. Filing mechanics

- District e-filing through the e-Courts/CIS portal; HC e-filing through the HC
  portal. Both change; confirm on the day.
- Certified copies from the Copying Agency of the court concerned.
- Court fee under the Rajasthan Court Fees and Suits Valuation Act 1961;
  e-stamp under the Rajasthan Stamp Act 1998.

## 8. The rule

Every intake ends with one of three words: **settled**, **open**, or
**conflicted**. Never with a forum alone.
