## Expected band distribution

Weeks 1–3: most cold attempts land at band 1–2. That is the design, not a
cohort problem.

Week 5–6: band 2–3. Anyone at band 4 cold by week 6 should be asked whether
they read ahead.

Week 8: band 3 minimum, band 1 is a fail.

## Traps that most often fail to catch

- TP-RJ-05's municipal notice — trainees forget it exists.
- TP-RJ-09's mutation conflict — trainees defend the affidavit.
- TP-RJ-11's clause — trainees read it as a defence almost every time.
- TP-RJ-24's thin reply — trainees under-write the won file.

If a cohort does not fall into these three, the cold prompts need sharpening.

Week 9 traps that most often fail to catch:

- The Art. 227 detour being treated as preserving limitation.
- s. 5 Limitation Act cited without checking it reaches revenue appeals.
- The receipt minimised rather than met on its own terms.
