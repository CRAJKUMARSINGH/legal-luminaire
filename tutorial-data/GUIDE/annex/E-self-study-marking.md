# Annex E — Self-Study Marking

Replaces session 3 in weeks 4–8 when you are working alone.

A partner catches your fluency. You will not. So this annex does three things:
it removes judgement from the first pass, it forces a delay before you mark,
and it makes you argue against yourself in writing.

**Do not skip the delay.** Immediate self-marking scores about a band high.

## The protocol

1. Finish session 1 and session 2. Do not open `expected`.
2. Set the artifact aside for **at least four hours**. Overnight is better.
3. Run the week's **presence checklist** below. Score literally — a criterion
   is met only if the artifact contains the thing, in words, on the page.
   Intention does not count. "I meant to" does not count.
4. Open `expected`. Run the **absence checklist**. Each hit costs a band.
5. Write the **self-challenge**: three sentences arguing that your own artifact
   is one band worse than you scored it.
6. Score the rewrite in session 4 separately, after another delay.

## Bias corrections

- **Fluency credit.** If a sentence reads well, check whether it is true before
  it earns anything.
- **Section sprinkling.** Any section number without a status tag is worth
  nothing, even if correct.
- **Checklist inflation.** If you ticked a criterion by paraphrasing, untick it
  and re-read the requirement.
- **Band creep.** Cap yourself at band 4 without a supervisor. Band 5 solo is
  self-flattery.

## Week 4 — Statutory clocks and replies

**Presence (1 point each, max 6)**
- [ ] Operative anchor document named (TP-RJ-02: the RPAD cover, not the notice)
- [ ] Anchor date written on the page
- [ ] Deadline computed from that anchor
- [ ] Days remaining stated
- [ ] Reply preserves the defence without a false admission
- [ ] TP-RJ-09: the affidavit conflict flagged rather than defended

**Absence (each hit −1 band)**
- Deadline computed from the notice date
- "Notice is bad for wrong address" pleaded without checking the address on the
  cheque
- Reply holds back waiting for completeness
- A paragraph that admits liability outright

## Week 5 — FIR, arrest and bail

**Presence (max 6)**
- [ ] Quash petition blocked, with a reason
- [ ] Interrogation brief written before any bail document
- [ ] s. 35(3) BNSS identified as the arrest question
- [ ] TP-RJ-06 sequence starts with certified copies
- [ ] TP-RJ-06 sequence starts with the jail vakalatnama
- [ ] s. 440 suspension placed before the appeal memo

**Absence**
- s. 528 BNSS filed or drafted as the primary remedy
- Merits argued inside a suspension application
- Brother's vakalatnama accepted without comment
- Grounds drafted before the record

## Week 6 — Pleadings, valuation and fee

**Presence (max 6)**
- [ ] Valuation basis named from the Act, not the deed
- [ ] Fee computed on that basis
- [ ] Fee section marked SECONDARY if unverified
- [ ] s. 207 pleaded in the plaint, not left to argument
- [ ] Municipal set-back notice addressed
- [ ] TP-RJ-11: five-year clause used as the client's limitation grant

**Absence**
- Suit valued at the 1998 consideration
- Injunction application with no khasra or property description
- Clause read as the developer's defence
- Set-back notice discovered late or ignored

## Week 7 — Appeals, limitation and delay

**Presence (max 6)**
- [ ] Limitation stated as a risk band, not a fact
- [ ] Delay affidavit blocked until dates exist
- [ ] Client's own follow-up record requisitioned
- [ ] Ground attacks the exclusion as a legal error
- [ ] Ground concedes the photocopy problem
- [ ] Appeal and s. 5 application filed together

**Absence**
- "Counsel failed to file" as the sole explanation
- Affidavit sworn from memory
- Ground re-argues weight of evidence
- Appeal held pending the predecessor's reply

## Week 8 — Mid-journey and capstone

**Presence (max 6)**
- [ ] No-objection stated as not required
- [ ] New vakalatnama filed without waiting
- [ ] Two separate vakalatnamas for two proceedings
- [ ] Depositions obtained from the copying agency
- [ ] Fee ledger requisitioned before any payment advice
- [ ] TP-RJ-10: "the compromise is probably binding" in the first three sentences

**Absence**
- Client advised to pay to obtain the file
- Single vakalatnama across both matters
- Bad news buried past paragraph three
- Undervaluation presented as a ground

## Week 9 — Revenue appeals

**Presence (max 7)**
- [ ] Board of Revenue named as the forum
- [ ] Art. 227 correctly characterised as supervisory, not substitutive
- [ ] Appeal provision tagged with a status
- [ ] Revenue limitation period identified
- [ ] Delay pleaded in the appeal, not only in a separate application
- [ ] Stay of implementation sought as a separate application
- [ ] s. 207 explained as the jurisdictional foundation

**Absence (each hit −1 band)**
- High Court named or implied as the forum
- Delay left to be inferred from the chronology
- Stay treated as a paragraph of the appeal
- Receipt omitted or mentioned without being met on its terms
- Civil court suggested as an alternative route

**Extra penalty.** This week carries a second band deduction, separate from the
absence list, for **filing blind**: an artifact that cites a provision it never
looked up.

## Week 9 middle rung

**Absence (each hit −1 band)**
- SDO's adverse recording not addressed
- Jamabandi produced without explaining the omission
- Additional evidence folded into the appeal instead of a separate application
- Reader allegation leading the grounds

## Week 10 — POCSO intake

**Presence (max 6)**
- [ ] Three columns separated: child / parent / record
- [ ] Statement position established before any bail position
- [ ] Sections read and their consequences stated
- [ ] Delay treated as weight, not limitation
- [ ] Support person and compensation raised
- [ ] Parent brief in plain Hindi, no section numbers

**Absence**
- Bail framed before sections read
- Three-week delay called a problem
- Child asked to repeat her account at the counter
- Accommodation request omitted

## Week 10 advanced rung

**Absence (each hit −1 band)**
- Gravity as the main ground
- Messages called tampering
- Refusal sought with no conditions
- Date discrepancy left for the defence
- No vakalatnama from the guardian

## Week 11 — POCSO trial

**Presence (max 7)**
- [ ] No leading question anywhere in chief
- [ ] Date discrepancy met in chief
- [ ] Screen application in writing, before the date
- [ ] Stamina plan with fixed breaks
- [ ] Mother's seating decided and reasoned
- [ ] Five-question 'do not ask' list
- [ ] Closing that lets the child leave

**Absence (each hit −1 band)**
- Screen left to the court to arrange
- Discrepancy left for cross
- Chief longer than fifteen minutes
- Mother seated directly behind the child
- Recall assumed

## The self-challenge

After scoring, write exactly three sentences:

> "This artifact is one band worse than I scored it because ___."
> "The sentence I am most pleased with is probably wrong because ___."
> "If a supervisor read only paragraph one, they would say ___."

If you cannot fill all three, you have not marked hard enough. Score down one
band and move to session 4.

## Solo debrief log

Facilitators keep a cohort debrief. You keep this instead — one line per week:

```
Week N | cold band | rewrite band | trap caught? Y/N | the sentence I will stop writing
```

The last column is the only one that compounds.
