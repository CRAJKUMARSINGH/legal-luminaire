# Annex G — Cold Prompts, Weeks 9–11

Three weeks that are not like the first eight. Read the week's setup before the
session, not during it.

Rules that apply throughout:

- Deliver the stimulus and then **stop talking**. No "have a look at the
  provisions". No pointing at the attachment.
- Every prompt below is timed. When the timer ends, take the paper whatever
  state it is in. A half-outline is data.
- The trainee may ask you anything. Answer only what a real client would know.
- Do not correct during a prompt. All correction happens in session 3.

## Week 9 — The revenue ladder, all four rungs

**This week is too heavy for one sitting.** Split it: prompts 1–2 in the
morning, prompts 3–4 after lunch. If you cannot split, cut prompt 4.

TP-RJ-13 is the map pair and may already have run as the week 8 capstone. If it
did, skip prompt 1.

### Setup

On the table: a stamped copy of a Collector's order, a certified order from an
SDO, a jamabandi extract, a blank sheet. Four different files, kept physically
separate. Do not stack them.

### Session 1 — cold

**Prompt 1 (TP-RJ-13) — 15 minutes.** "Draw the revenue ladder for this district
on the board. Every rung, who sits on it, and what each one can do. Do it from
memory, then open TP-RJ-13 and mark what you got wrong."

*Trap: a flat ladder, or the Collector placed where the SDO sits. Watch whether
they draw the Board at all.*

**Prompt 2 (TP-RJ-14) — 20 minutes.** Hand over the stamped order, not the
certified copy. "The appeal has to go this week. Grounds, now."

*Trap: drafting begins. Nobody asks for the certified copy, nobody works out
how much of the period is left. Watch the first ninety seconds.*

**Prompt 3 (TP-RJ-16) — 30 minutes.** Certified order, jamabandi extract. "The
SDO recorded my client's statement and then ignored it. Grounds for the
Collector."

*Trap: the recording goes unmentioned because it is in the middle of the order;
or the reader-bribery allegation is promoted to ground one with no date and no
figure.*

**Prompt 4 (TP-RJ-24) — 25 minutes.** "The Collector decided in our favour last
month. The brother has gone to the Commissioner. Date is next month. What do we
file?"

*Trap: "nothing, we won." Watch for the reply that never gets written.*

### Session 2 — supervised

One file each. Certified copy application for the TP-RJ-14 matter, grounds
skeleton for TP-RJ-16, and the TP-RJ-24 reply outline. You read the TP-RJ-14 and
TP-RJ-24 work only; TP-RJ-16 goes to the group.

### Session 3 — reveal

Open Annex D at week 9. Work the trap index F rows in order, and do not read
the counter-move until the trainee has said whether they fell in.

Two questions to ask out loud before any counter-move:

- "Which of these four files is the one where doing nothing loses it?"
- "What did you ask for that you did not get?"

### If you have one hour

Prompt 2, then prompt 4. The clock and the win. Skip the map.

### Failure signals

- Prompt 2 grounds written before the copy is asked for.
- Prompt 3 leading with the bribe.
- Prompt 4 answered in one line.
- Any prompt where the Board of Revenue is named without being asked for.

## Week 10 — POCSO, both postures

**Run TP-RJ-15 and TP-RJ-17 in the same session, in that order, with a
ten-minute gap.** Do not split across days. The point is that the second one
inverts the first.

### Setup

A single file, opened once, on the table. FIR copy, the child's 183 statement,
a message chronology. The same papers serve both prompts — do not swap them out.

### Session 1 — cold

**Prompt 1 (TP-RJ-15) — 25 minutes.** "Bail application is listed tomorrow. The
family is here. Draft the opposition."

*Traps, in the order they usually appear: the application is opposed before the
FIR's sections are read; delay is called fatal; the parent's account is written
down as the child's.*

**Ten-minute gap.** Collect the paper. Do not discuss it. Ask only: "Whose
words are in your third paragraph?"

**Prompt 2 (TP-RJ-17) — 30 minutes.** "Different family, three days later. He
has bail now. They want it refused. Same papers — go."

*Traps: gravity offered as the ground; the messages described as tampering
without one message being quoted; refusal prayed for with no conditions named.*

### Session 2 — supervised

Set the two drafts side by side and make the trainee mark every place the
posture changed and every place it should have. Then the refusal-with-conditions
paragraph, redone.

### Session 3 — reveal

Annex D week 10. The first question is not about either draft:

- "What did you assume about the child that the papers do not say?"

Then the trap index, counter-moves last.

### If you have one hour

Prompt 2 only, and take the conditions paragraph through to a finished form.

### Failure signals

- Prompt 1: any reference to a limitation period.
- Prompt 2: the word "serious" as a ground.
- Both: the child's age stated as a number the trainee has not read.
- Prompt 2 answered by re-arguing prompt 1.

## Week 11 — The examination

**Three waves, one file, one sitting.** Each wave changes the document the
trainee is holding. Take the paper between waves and keep it.

### Setup

The child is coming on Thursday. A screen is available. The mother is present.
Nothing is decided yet.

### Session 1 — cold

**Wave 1 (TP-RJ-18) — 20 minutes.** "Examination-in-chief outline. She is
fourteen. Twenty minutes of her, no more."

*Traps: leading questions; the date discrepancy left for cross; an outline that
runs past twenty minutes.*

**Wave 2 — 10 minutes.** "Now: where does everyone sit, and what do you file
for the screen?"

*Trap: the screen left to the court's discretion; the mother seated behind the
child.*

**Wave 3 — 10 minutes.** "The date conflict is real. Rewrite the part of the
outline that touches it."

*Trap: the discrepancy moved into cross instead of being met in chief.*

### Session 2 — supervised

Read the outline aloud, you as the child. Stop at the first leading question and
make them find the rest. Then time the read.

### Session 3 — reveal

Annex D week 11. Before any counter-move, ask:

- "How many of your questions could she answer with one word?"
- "What does the mother's face do to her answer?"

### If you have one hour

Waves 1 and 3. Skip the seating, but say out loud that you skipped it.

### Failure signals

- More than two leading questions in the first five minutes.
- No written screen application.
- A chief that runs past twenty-five minutes when read aloud.
- The discrepancy absent from the outline entirely.
- Seating decided after the outline rather than before it.
