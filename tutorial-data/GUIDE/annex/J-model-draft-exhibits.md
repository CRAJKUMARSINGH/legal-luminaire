# Annex J — Model Draft Exhibits

Trainee-facing, with one hard rule: **an exhibit is not a draft.** Each exhibit
below shows what a document looks like *after* the gates have cleared and a
supervising advocate has signed off. It is a ceiling to aim at, not a template
to copy. Nothing here is filing-ready, and no exhibit may be exported from the
workbench while any `PENDING` row remains in the pair's verification block.

## Exhibit J-1 — Regular bail, Sessions Court (s. 483 BNSS)

Companion to the week-5 posture. Structure to recognise, not text to reuse.

```
IN THE COURT OF THE LEARNED SESSIONS JUDGE, UDAIPUR

Criminal Miscellaneous Bail Application No. ____ of 2026
FIR No. 142/2026, PS Mavli, Udaipur
Under Sections: 279 & 304A IPC / corresponding BNS provisions, and MV Act

Shri Ramesh Chandra Meena                    ... Applicant / Accused
        Versus
State of Rajasthan                           ... Respondent

APPLICATION UNDER SECTION 483 BNSS, 2023
(CORRESPONDING TO SECTION 439 CrPC) FOR GRANT OF REGULAR BAIL

1. The applicant is in judicial custody since 14.11.2026 in the FIR
   noted above.

2. [Gate banner — this paragraph carries no citation until every row in
   the pair's verification block is unblocked. The engine's draft output
   inserts: "Citations withheld pending verification — see gate report."]

3. Facts, in the client's own order: tractor-trolley on the NH-8 Mavli
   stretch, 12.11.2026, about 7:45 PM; a motorcycle from the opposite
   direction; the rider later succumbed; the applicant stopped, informed
   the police, cooperated.

4. Grounds, in the order the gate engine permits them:
   (a) no criminal antecedents;
   (b) fixed abode, family roots, two minor children;
   (c) cooperation after the incident, documented;
   (d) the offence, at its highest, does not attract the heinous-case
       exception — [PENDING: named authority required before this
       sentence may carry a citation].

5. Prayer: release on bail, subject to conditions this Hon'ble Court
   deems fit; local sureties offered.

6. Verification: signed by the applicant — never by a relative.

Place: Udaipur                            Advocate for the applicant
Date:  ______
```

**What to notice.** The citation paragraph is a hole on purpose. In the
repository's posture, a draft with an unfilled gate is an *honest* draft; a
draft with a filled-in guess is the product this course exists to prevent.

## Exhibit J-2 — s. 138 reply, the security-cheque framing

Companion to TP-RJ-02. The reply's skeleton, not its rhetoric:

```
1. Receipt of the notice on [date fixed from the RPAD cover — not the
   notice date] is admitted.
2. The signature on the cheque is admitted. [Say it once, plainly.]
3. The cheque was handed as security under the agreement to sell dated
   [date]; no debt was due on the date of presentation.
4. The agreement stands cancelled by [correspondence], and the
   consideration has failed.
5. Without prejudice: the complainant is put to strict proof of a
   legally enforceable debt subsisting on the date of presentation.
6. [The unstamped-agreement point is NOT argued here. Flag it to the
   supervisor; impounding is a trial-stage problem to pre-empt, not a
   reply-stage flourish.]
```

**What to notice.** No instalment offer, no tender, no over-argument on service.
The strategic fork in TP-RJ-04 belongs to the client's written instruction, not
to this document.

## Exhibit J-3 — The bilingual habit

Every exhibit above has a Hindi twin, written in Hindi and not translated from
English. Two sentences of the s. 138 reply, done properly:

> प्राप्ति की तिथि [आर.पी.ए.डी. लिफाफे से] स्वीकार है। चेक पर हस्ताक्षर स्वीकार है, पर
> चेक सुरक्षा-स्वरूप दिया गया था; प्रस्तुति की तिथि कोई विधिक देय राशि बाकी नहीं थी।

**What to notice.** "सुरक्षा-स्वरूप", "विधिक देय", "प्रस्तुति की तिथि" — these are
the words a Jaipur draftsman actually writes. A translated "the cheque was given
as a security guarantee" reads as translation, and translation reads as
insecurity at a district counter.

## End of the guide

The guide is complete when the corpus is complete: twenty-four pairs, eleven
weeks, ten annexes, and two verification cards still open. Before any cohort
touches weeks 9–11, sign V-01 and V-02 in `VERIFY/` and run the calibration
session once, facilitators only.

A revenue file is lost by the gap you left, not by the argument you lost.
Every word she says is a word she has to carry. Say fewer of them.
