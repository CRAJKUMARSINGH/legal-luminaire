# Calibration Run — Criterion 7

Ninety minutes. Facilitators only. No trainees. Run once per panel, before any
cohort reaches week 11, and again after any change to the criterion 7 anchors.

## Why

A criterion with prose anchors drifts. Two facilitators twelve months apart will
band the same chief two bands apart unless they have sat in a room and disagreed
out loud first.

## Materials

- Annex I, printed. Do not distribute before minute 0.
- The week 11 sheet, printed, one per facilitator.
- Two blank sheets for the re-mark.

## Run

**0–10. Read the facts only.** Facts block and nothing else. Then close it.

**10–35. Mark Document A blind.** From the facts block alone — no notes, no
Document B. Use the sheet. Time it yourself.

**35–45. Compare.** Put every score on the wall, sub-item by sub-item. Do not
argue yet. Record the spread per sub-item.

**45–60. Argue.** Now read Annex I's notes. Work the sub-items with the widest
spread first. The question is never "which of us is right" — it is "what in the
paper did the outlier see or miss".

**60–70. Mark Document B.** Same conditions. This is the check: anyone who
banded A high will band B high too, and the gap should be two bands.

**70–85. Reconcile.** Read Annex I's band for both. Anyone more than one band
off on either document re-marks a live week 11 file with a second facilitator
before week 11 runs.

**85–90. Close.** One line each on which sub-item they personally under-weight.
Write them on the wall and leave them up for the cohort.

## What to expect

- **A will be marked too high.** Most real chiefs read like A. The usual landing
  is 3, occasionally 4, and the argument is always "it would work in court".
  Answer with Q7 and Q13, not with theory.
- **The accommodation items will be skipped** by at least one facilitator because
  they are not on the page in Document A. That is the finding, not a distraction.
- **Someone will argue the child's age makes open questions unfair.** It does the
  opposite. A fourteen-year-old gives a better open account than an adult and is
  more easily led.

## Fail conditions

Re-run the whole session if any of these happen:

- The median band for A is 4 or above
- Nobody scored 7f or 7g below 3 on A
- The spread on 7a exceeds two bands
- Anyone banded B below A

## Recording

Log the panel's spread per sub-item in `VERIFY/log.md` under a calibration
heading. The spread is the thing that improves; the bands are only the occasion.
