# Week 1 — Intake Triage

**Level** beginner · **Pairs** TP-RJ-01, TP-RJ-10 · **Time** 4 × 45 min
**Prerequisite** none. Everyone does this week.

## Why this week exists

A client walks in with an oral story and no papers and asks "kya karein". The
beginner answer is a document. The correct answer is usually "not yet, and here
is exactly what I need". This week builds the refusal.

It is week 1 because everything downstream — forum, clock, draft — is guesswork
until triage is done. A trainee who cannot refuse cannot be trusted with a
forum.

## Objectives

1. Classify urgency, document completeness and request clarity without being
   talked into a higher urgency than the file supports.
2. Write a triage memo that names what is missing, not what might be done.
3. Say "not yet" to a client who is asking for a fight.

## Reading

Nothing statutory. Read Annex C (glossary) and Annex A (forum map) sections 1
and 2 only.

## Session 1 — Cold attempt (45 min)

Open `pairs/TP-RJ-01-kabza-forum-split.json`. Copy only the `input` block.
Expected block sealed.

Write, in this order:
- one-line read of the file
- urgency, completeness, clarity, with a reason for each
- the three things you would need before naming a forum

Then open `pairs/TP-RJ-10-compromise-second-opinion.json`. Same drill. 20
minutes each.

## Session 2 — Gate prediction (30 min)

Without looking at `expected`, write your predicted `citationGate`,
`limitationGate` and `terminalOutcome.kind` for both pairs, and one sentence of
reasoning each.

Then write the number of things you listed as missing for TP-RJ-01. Compare it
later.

## Session 3 — Reveal and mark (60 min)

Open both `expected` blocks. Mark your session 1 output against the Rubric
with a partner. Mark honestly — band 1 is a legitimate score this week.

Count how many of the engine's `checklist` items you actually requisitioned.
Most trainees get two of eight. That gap is the week.

## Session 4 — Rewrite (45 min)

Rewrite the TP-RJ-01 memo at band 4. You must:
- state the forum is *not yet decidable* and give the reason
- name the revenue-versus-civil split as an issue, not a conclusion
- produce a requisition list of at least six items

You may not use the word "cheating", "fraud" or "strong case".

## Failure modes this week catches

- **Premature forum.** Picking civil court because the tehsildar said so.
- **Client-echo.** Repeating "notice is bad for wrong address" because the
  client said it, without checking. (TP-RJ-10's version: "I was cheated".)
- **Urgency inflation.** Marking TP-RJ-10 critical because the client is upset.
- **Document blindness.** Writing a draft for a file with no khasra.
- **False comfort.** Telling a client who was underpaid that undervaluation is a
  ground.

## Deliverable

The rewritten TP-RJ-01 memo, band 4, plus a two-line log of your cold band and
what changed.

## Carry-forward

Week 2 assumes you can hold a file at "forum undecided". If you forced a forum
this week, week 2 will feel like repetition. It is not; it is the correction.
