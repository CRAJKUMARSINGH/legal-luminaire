# Week 2 — Forum and Jurisdiction

**Level** beginner · **Pairs** TP-RJ-01, TP-RJ-05 · **Time** 4 × 60 min
**Prerequisite** week 1.

## Why this week exists

Forum is the single most expensive beginner error in Rajasthan practice. File a
khatedari dispute in civil court and you lose two years to a jurisdiction
objection before anyone reads your facts. This week makes forum a conscious
decision with a stated reason, every time.

## Objectives

1. Identify, for any intake, whether the forum question is settled, open or
   conflicted — and say which.
2. State the bar to civil jurisdiction and the exception to it, in one sentence
   each, without overstating either.
3. Distinguish pecuniary, territorial and subject-matter jurisdiction as three
   separate questions.

## Reading

Annex A in full. Bare Act: Rajasthan Tenancy Act 1955 s. 207, and CPC ss. 9,
15–20. Read s. 207 twice; it is four lines and decides more files than any
judgment.

## Session 1 — Cold attempt (60 min)

TP-RJ-01 again, but this time the output is a **forum memo only** — no triage,
no requisition. One page:

- which forums are theoretically available
- which is barred and on what basis
- which exception, if any, saves the civil suit
- what single fact would settle the question

Then TP-RJ-05, same drill. Note that TP-RJ-05's forum *is* settled and the work
is knowing why — that contrast is the lesson.

## Session 2 — Three jurisdictions (45 min)

Take TP-RJ-05 and split jurisdiction three ways: subject-matter, territorial,
pecuniary. For each, write what the file already answers and what it does not.
Pecuniary will force you into valuation, which week 6 will collect on.

## Session 3 — Reveal and mark (60 min)

Open both expected blocks. Mark session 1. The engine refuses to pick a forum on
TP-RJ-01 and picks on TP-RJ-05 — check whether your reasoning matched the
*reasons*, not the answers.

## Session 4 — Rewrite (60 min)

Rewrite the TP-RJ-01 forum memo at band 4. Requirements:
- name s. 207 and state the bar accurately
- name at least one exception and say whether it applies on these facts
- conclude "not yet decidable" with the settling fact identified

## Failure modes this week catches

- **Forum by rumour.** Tehsildar, patwari, or a cousin's advice.
- **Bar-and-exception collapse.** Writing "civil court has no jurisdiction"
  flatly, so the plaint has no answer when the bar is raised.
- **Conflating the three jurisdictions.** Getting subject-matter right and
  territorial wrong, which is still a return.
- **Settled-question blindness.** Over-thinking a file where forum is obvious
  and the real work is elsewhere.

## Deliverable

The rewritten forum memo, plus a one-page note on the three jurisdictions using
TP-RJ-05.

## Carry-forward

Week 3 requisitions documents. What you requisition must follow from the forum
you chose. If your requisition list would be the same in civil court and in
revenue court, you have not understood this week.
