# Week 7 — Appeals, Limitation and Delay

**Level** advanced · **Pairs** TP-RJ-06, TP-RJ-07 · **Time** 4 × 75 min
**Prerequisite** weeks 1–6.

## Why this week exists

A delay application is where good appeals go to die. This week teaches the
distinction that decides them: client inaction is usually fatal, counsel
inaction is forgivable, and the two get mixed by people who have not built a
record.

## Objectives

1. Compute appeal limitation from the correct date and state it as a risk band,
   not a fact.
2. Build a day-by-day delay explanation from the client's own record.
3. Frame a ground on a legal error rather than on weight of evidence.

## Reading

Limitation Act ss. 5, Arts. 115, 116. CPC s. 96, O. 41 r. 1. BNSS ss. 415,
440. Annex B. Read s. 5 once and note how short it is — the whole doctrine is
in the word "sufficient".

## Session 1 — Cold attempt (75 min)

TP-RJ-07: draft the s. 5 application's opening three paragraphs and the client
affidavit's structure. You do not have the follow-up dates; work out what you
would need.

TP-RJ-06: draft the first two grounds only.

## Session 2 — The record (45 min)

For TP-RJ-07, list every piece of evidence that would turn "counsel did not
file" from an assertion into a case. Mark which the client can produce today.

## Session 3 — Reveal and mark (solo)

Working alone? Do not open `expected` yet. Set the artifact aside for four
hours minimum, then run the week's presence and absence checklists in
Annex E. Score literally. Write the three-sentence self-challenge before you
open `expected`.

With a partner, mark in pairs against the Rubric and compare bands before
discussing.

## Session 4 — Rewrite (75 min)

Rewrite the TP-RJ-07 first ground at band 4. It must attack the *exclusion* of
the 1987 settlement as a legal error, and must acknowledge that the document is
a photocopy.

## Failure modes this week catches

- **One-line delay.** "Counsel failed to file" with nothing behind it.
- **Affidavit from memory.** Swearing dates nobody checked.
- **Weight-based grounds.** Attacking the finding instead of the reasoning.
- **Waiting for the predecessor.** Holding the appeal for a reply that may never
  come.

## Deliverable

The rewritten ground, the evidence list, and the delay application opening.

## Carry-forward

Week 8 hands you a file someone else already broke. Everything in weeks 1–7 is
assumed and none of it is stated.
