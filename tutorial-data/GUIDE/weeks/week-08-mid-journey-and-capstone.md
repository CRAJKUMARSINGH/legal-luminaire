# Week 8 — Mid-Journey and Capstone

**Level** expert · **Pairs** TP-RJ-08, TP-RJ-10, all twenty-four · **Time** 4 × 75 min
**Prerequisite** weeks 1–7.

## Why this week exists

Most training assumes a clean file at intake. Real files arrive mid-stream, with
a predecessor who is not answering, a client who has already signed something,
and a date on the board. This week is the capstone because it assumes all of it
at once and gives you no clean start.

## Objectives

1. Take over a file without the predecessor's cooperation and without losing a
   date.
2. Tell a client honestly that the thing he wants undone probably cannot be.
3. Audit your own eight weeks against the corpus.

## Reading

CPC O. 3 r. 4. Nothing else new. Re-read Annex D only after session 1.

## Session 1 — Cold attempt (75 min)

TP-RJ-08: write the handover audit. Two columns — recover, and from where. Then
answer the client's actual question: is a no-objection required?

TP-RJ-10: write the second-opinion memo. Start with the answer the client will
not like.

## Session 2 — Both matters (45 min)

TP-RJ-08 has a 498A trial and a DV Act proceeding. Confirm they need separate
vakalatnamas and write the court-readiness list for 4 December.

TP-RJ-10: read the order sheet question — why does the whole memo depend on
whether a decree exists?

## Session 3 — Reveal and mark (solo)

Working alone? Do not open `expected` yet. Set the artifact aside for four
hours minimum, then run the week's presence and absence checklists in
Annex E. Score literally. Write the three-sentence self-challenge before you
open `expected`.

With a partner, mark in pairs against the Rubric and compare bands before
discussing.

## Session 4 — Capstone audit (75 min)

Take any two pairs you have not used. Cold-write the triage and the gates only —
no document. Mark yourself. Report your band on each of the six criteria across
the whole course.

## Failure modes this week catches

- **Waiting for the no-objection.** Losing a date to a courtesy.
- **Fee-lien panic.** Advising the client to pay whatever is demanded before
  reading a ledger.
- **Soft bad news.** Burying "the compromise is binding" in paragraph nine.
- **Single vakalatnama.** Leaving one of two matters unrepresented.

## Deliverable

The handover audit, the second-opinion memo, and a one-page self-audit with
bands across all six criteria.

## Course exit

Band 3 average across the six criteria, with no band 1 at week 8. Below that,
repeat weeks 6–8. Above band 4, you are ready to supervise a beginner through
weeks 1–3.
