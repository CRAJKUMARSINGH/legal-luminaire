# Week 9 — Revenue Appeals to the Board of Revenue

**Level** expert · **Pair** TP-RJ-13 · **Time** 4 × 75 min
**Prerequisite** weeks 1–8.

## Why this week exists

The revenue ladder is the part of Rajasthan practice that civil-trained
advocates get wrong most often and most expensively. It is also the ladder where
advocates who should be in revenue court file a writ instead, and burn the
client's limitation doing it.

Week 9 sits after the capstone because it is the purest test of week 2: the bar
in s. 207 of the Tenancy Act is not an obstacle to route around, it is the
reason the revenue forum exists.

## Objectives

1. Place a matter on the revenue ladder correctly — Tehsildar, SDO, Collector,
   Divisional Commissioner, Board of Revenue — and name the appeal provision.
2. Distinguish a revenue appeal from an Art. 227 petition and say why the wrong
   choice is not merely inefficient but fatal.
3. Handle a revenue limitation period, which is short and unforgiving, and a
   stay of implementation that has to be sought separately.

## Reading

Rajasthan Land Revenue Act 1956 — the appeal chapter and the stay provision.
Rajasthan Tenancy Act 1955 s. 207 and the partition provisions. Art. 227 of the
Constitution. Annex B closing note on state Acts.

Section numbers in the Land Revenue Act chapter are marked `PENDING` in the pair
because they vary by amendment. **Read the bare Act before session 1 and write
the numbers you actually find.** If you cannot find them, that is the lesson.

## Two rungs — and a middle

Week 9 has three rungs. Do the one that matches your level.

- **Beginner rung (TP-RJ-14):** the appeal is not drafted. You triage, you find
  the period, you requisition. Output: memo and list.
- **Middle rung (TP-RJ-16):** the SDO has already ruled against the client and
  written the client's own defects into the order. Output: appeal memo opening
  and an additional-evidence application. Do this rung if you have done the
  beginner rung and want the next one up.
- **Expert rung (TP-RJ-13):** the appeal is late and a receipt may have killed
  it. Output: appeal, stay and condonation.

The beginner rung is week 9's first three sessions only. Sessions 1 and 2 become:
read the order photo, compute the period from the bare Act, write the copying
application and the requisition list. Session 4 becomes: write the three-sentence
opening of the appeal memo — forum, order appealed, period position.

## Session 1 — Cold attempt (75 min)

Open the pair for your rung. Input only.

Write:
- the correct forum and the appeal provision, with a status tag
- what the earlier advocate did
- whether any delay needs its own application, and on what record

At the expert rung, add: what the receipt says, in the client's words, and what
the other side will say it means.

## Session 2 — The record (45 min)

List every document the appeal needs and where each comes from. Mark the one
that must be applied for today.

## Session 3 — Reveal and mark (solo)

Working alone? Run Annex E — the week 9 block, and the week 9 middle-rung
block if you took TP-RJ-16. Score literally, after the four-hour delay.

With a partner, mark against the Rubric.

## Session 4 — Rewrite (75 min)

At the expert rung: rewrite the appeal's opening three paragraphs so that the
delay is conceded in the first one and the receipt is met in the second.
At the middle rung: the appeal memo opening and the additional-evidence
application, separate documents.
At the beginner rung: the copying application and the requisition list.

## Failure modes this week catches

- **Filing blind.** Citing a provision you never looked up.
- **Drafting before copying.** Grounds begun with no certified order.
- **Civil court reflex.** A writ or suit where a revenue appeal lies.
- **Delay left to inference.** A chronology that expects the reader to compute.
- **Win treated as won.** (TP-RJ-24's lesson landing early.)
- **Receipt minimised.** Treating the signed receipt as a formality.

## Deliverable

The rung's output plus a one-line log: the period you found in the Act, and the
page you found it on.

## Carry-forward

Week 10 changes the subject entirely. The revenue ladder stays on your shelf;
you will need it again at week 9b and in the capstone audit.

## Beginner rung presence checklist

- [ ] Appeal period found in the bare Act and diarised
- [ ] Copying application written before any grounds
- [ ] SDO named as the forum, not the civil court
- [ ] Mutation-versus-title distinction stated
- [ ] Notice-absence pleaded as procedural, not assumed
- [ ] Certified order requisitioned

## Middle rung presence checklist

- [ ] SDO's adverse recording about the jamabandi met head-on
- [ ] Jamabandi produced with the omission explained
- [ ] Additional-evidence application filed separately
- [ ] Collector named as the forum
- [ ] Reader allegation kept to one paragraph or dropped
- [ ] Stay considered if possession is disturbed
