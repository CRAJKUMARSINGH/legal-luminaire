# Week 10 — POCSO and Child-Victim Intake

**Level** intermediate · **Pair** TP-RJ-15 · **Time** 4 × 75 min
**Prerequisite** week 5.

## Why this week exists

POCSO intake fails differently. The parent asks about bail, the child is in the
room, and the advocate answers the wrong question in front of the person least
able to absorb it. This week teaches the order: statement, sections, child,
then bail.

## Objectives

1. Separate the child's words from the parent's words from the record.
2. Establish the statement position before framing any bail position.
3. Brief a parent in plain Hindi without frightening the child.

## Reading

POCSO Act ss. 3, 4, 7, 8, 11, 12, 19, 24, 33(5), 33(8), 36, 39. BNSS s. 183.
Annex B. Read s. 33(5) and s. 36 together — they exist to stop the child being
called again.

## Session 1 — Cold attempt (75 min)

TP-RJ-15. Input only. Write the triage memo. In it:
- the three columns (child's words / mother's words / counsellor's note)
- what you would establish before taking any bail position
- the three questions you would ask the mother and the one you would not ask
  the child

## Session 2 — Sections (45 min)

Read the FIR's sections. Write what each one requires and what the fallback is
if the assault allegation thins. Then write the bail position you would take,
and the reason it might be the wrong one.

## Session 3 — Reveal and mark (solo)

Solo: Annex E week 10. Partner: the Rubric.

## Session 4 — Rewrite (75 min)

Rewrite the parent brief at band 4. Requirements:
- plain Hindi, no section numbers in the body
- what happens next, in order
- what the child will face, honestly
- what the parent should and should not do
- one sentence on school

## Failure modes this week catches

- **Bail-first.** Framing opposition before reading the section.
- **Voice confusion.** The parent's account written as the child's account.
- **Delay panic.** Treating three weeks as a limitation problem.
- **Counter examination.** Making the child repeat her account in the waiting
  room.
- **Missing infrastructure.** No support person, no compensation, no
  accommodation request.

## Deliverable

The parent brief and the three-column note.

## Course exit

Band 3. Week 10 is optional for the certificate and mandatory before a first
POCSO file unsupervised.

## Advanced rung — TP-RJ-17

If you have run TP-RJ-15, take TP-RJ-17 in a second pass. The bench has leaned.
Sessions 1 and 2 become: read the 183 statement and the messages, write the
concession list, then write the conditions package. Session 4 becomes the reply
affidavit's closing three paragraphs.

Advanced rung presence checklist:

- [ ] Gravity not used as the main ground
- [ ] Medical corroboration dismissed as a bail factor, explicitly
- [ ] Messages framed as access, not tampering
- [ ] Date discrepancy raised by us first
- [ ] 'Months' assertion tested against the board
- [ ] Conditions offered in the alternative to refusal
- [ ] Vakalatnama from the guardian obtained
