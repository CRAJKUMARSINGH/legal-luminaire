# Week 11 — POCSO Trial and Examination

**Level** expert · **Pair** TP-RJ-18 · **Time** 4 × 90 min
**Prerequisite** week 10, both rungs.

## Why this week exists

Bail is a hearing. Trial is a year. Week 10 taught you to keep the child out of
the corridor; week 11 teaches you to get her through the box once, well, and not
again. That is the whole skill and it is mostly preparation, not performance.

## Objectives

1. Build an examination-in-chief that is chronological, non-leading, and short
   enough for a fourteen-year-old's stamina.
2. Secure the s. 36 screen and the accommodation before the date, in writing.
3. Decide what not to ask, and hold that line under a hostile cross.

## Reading

POCSO ss. 33(5), 36, 42A. Evidence law on leading questions in chief —
BSA 2023 ss. 138–143 (verify numbering). Annex B. Read s. 33(5) again: it is
the provision that decides how many times you get.

## Session 1 — Cold attempt (90 min)

TP-RJ-18. Input only. Write, in Hindi:
- the examination-in-chief outline, in the child's order of events
- the five questions you will not ask
- the stamina plan — where the breaks fall

## Session 2 — The screen and the room (60 min)

Write the s. 36 accommodation application. Then write the practical note: who
sits where, who the child can see, whether the mother is in the room or out of
it, and why. Then read your own outline and cut it by a third.

## Session 3 — Reveal and mark (solo)

Solo: Annex E week 11. Partner: the Rubric.

## Session 4 — Rewrite (90 min)

Rewrite the outline at band 4. Requirements:
- no leading question anywhere in chief
- the date discrepancy met in chief, not left for cross
- the counsellor's note anchored to the child's own words
- a closing that lets the child leave rather than trailing off

## Failure modes this week catches

- **Leading in chief.** "You were assaulted, correct?" — the commonest POCSO
  trial error and the one that hands the defence its first point.
- **Own-goal dates.** Leaving the inconsistency for cross.
- **Endurance blindness.** A forty-minute chief for a child who manages twelve.
- **Screen left to the court.** No written request, so none is made.
- **Mother in the room.** Comfort that becomes a prompt.
- **Recall waste.** Asking for a recall that s. 33(5) will not give.

## Deliverable

The rewritten outline, the accommodation application, and the stamina plan.

## Course exit

Band 3. Week 11 is optional for the certificate. Do not take a POCSO trial
unsupervised without it and a facilitator who has stood through one.
